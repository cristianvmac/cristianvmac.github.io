For small businesses, social networks are a way to increase visibility and specifically reach the target audience. However, since there are many platforms, it may be challenging to choose which ones to focus on.

According to the Best Social Media Platforms Guide , there are four best social media platforms for small businesses: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Google Business Profile. Therefore, depending on the business, industry, and customers’ preferences, small businesses should pick the best social media platforms for their needs.

## 1. Facebook

**Best for:** Local community building, broad demographic reach, and targeted advertising.

Facebook remains one of the most practical platforms for small business marketing. It is still the most popular platform worldwide, giving small businesses access to a wide audience across age groups and interests. For local businesses, Facebook offers tools for building community through local groups and events, while its advertising options are cost-effective and highly targeted. This makes Facebook a strong choice for businesses that want to stay connected with their local audience and reach new customers without a large ad budget.

## 2. Instagram

**Best for:** Visual brands, e-commerce, and product discovery.

Instagram is especially valuable for businesses that rely on strong visuals, such as retail, food, fashion, beauty, home goods, and creative services. More than 80% of shopping enthusiasts use Instagram to discover new products, making it a powerful platform for product discovery and e-commerce. Features like Reels, Stories, and built-in shopping tags allow businesses to showcase products, tell stories, and make it easier for customers to move from inspiration to purchase.

## 3. LinkedIn

**Best for:** B2B companies, consulting, and professional services.

LinkedIn is the go-to platform for businesses that sell to other businesses or offer professional services. It connects companies directly with decision-makers, industry leaders, and potential B2B clients. For consultants, agencies, and professional service providers, LinkedIn helps build brand authority and credibility by sharing expertise, engaging in industry conversations, and developing relationships with the right professional audience.

## 4. Google Business Profile

**Best for:** Local search visibility and the “Map Pack.”

Google Business Profile is technically a local directory rather than a traditional social media platform, but it can be used like a social feed to support local visibility. By posting updates, collecting reviews, and keeping business information accurate, small businesses can capture high-intent buyers who are actively searching for products or services “near me.” A well-maintained Google Business Profile can help a business appear in local search results and the “Map Pack,” making it a valuable tool for local discovery.

## Choosing the Right Platforms

The best platform for your small business depends on your goals. Facebook is ideal for broad reach and local community engagement. Instagram works well for visual storytelling and e-commerce. LinkedIn is strongest for B2B relationships and professional authority. Google Business Profile is essential for local search visibility and attracting nearby customers.

Rather than trying to be everywhere, small businesses should focus on the platforms where their audience is most active and where their content can deliver the most value. Used strategically, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Google Business Profile can help small businesses build awareness, connect with customers, and grow.
